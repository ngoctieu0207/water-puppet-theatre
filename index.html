Hý Thủy · a digital water puppet theatre

Water Puppetry

Múa Rối Nước

Hý Thủy (戲水) means “playing in the water”, capturing the joy and playfulness of movement on the water. Inspired by Vietnam's traditional water puppetry, Hý Thủy is an interactive digital stage where you can explore the art of water puppetry, discover its characters, and bring wooden puppets to life through your own performances.

What is water puppetry?

The art form

What is water puppetry?

Water puppetry, múa rối nước in Vietnamese, is a folk theatre where the stage is a pool of water. Lacquered wooden puppets glide, spin and splash across the surface. The puppeteers stand hidden in the water behind a bamboo blind and move them with long rods and strings under the surface, to live folk music and drums.

Where does it come from?

It grew up in the villages of the Red River Delta in northern Vietnam, where life revolves around ponds and flooded rice fields. Villagers performed it at festivals, with scenes of farming, fishing, children at play and legendary creatures such as the dragon and the phoenix.

How would you like to perform?

Choose your puppet

Each puppet has its own way of moving and its own signature move on E.

Tell a story

Write a few short sentences: who does what, and to whom. The puppets act it out and the music follows the mood (a fight gets battle drums, a night scene gets a quiet tune). English or Vietnamese both work.

Characters:
Actions: appear · swim · jump · dive · hide · rise · greet / bow · spin · dance / splash / spray · fight · go to / visit … · hold hands with … · love / hug · forbid / scold · cry · laugh · ride … · fly to the sky · leave
Linking words: then · but · and · they · everyone (rồi · nhưng · họ · tất cả)

How to Play

You are the hidden puppeteer. Move your puppet across the water, make it rise and sink, and string the moves together into a little scene.

Move

  • WASD Glide across the water (hold)
  • ↑↓ Rise / sink
  • ←→ Turn

Play

  • Space Jump (spins if you are moving)
  • Q Greet
  • E Signature move
  • F Dive under and come back up

Reset, sound and pause are the buttons at the top of the screen.
